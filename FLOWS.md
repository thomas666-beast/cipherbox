# Standard Cipherbox verification

- Register and sign in with a password. Verify protected routes and the admin panel.
- Create a room, send a message, open members, and discover public rooms. Older history should stay in order, without duplicates.
- On Rooms, load more and switch room-type filters. Filters must cover results beyond the first page.
- In Library, browse roots and subcategories, use breadcrumb links, and load more items. Direct category URLs must open without fetching the entire tree. Admins can still create a category and upload, rename, and download files.
- In Admin, open a report and page through the queue; the read-only room viewer can load older messages.
